## Dialogs
- role: neutral/positive style adaptation
- train: 6433
- validation: 43
- test: 55
- total: 6531 utterances (~9.98 h)
- SID0 = neutral
- SID1 = positive

Both Dialogs conditioning groups contain both Masha and Sveta, so SID0/SID1 are style labels rather than actor IDs.
